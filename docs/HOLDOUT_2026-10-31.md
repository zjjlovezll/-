# M7 step 3: hold-outs — one amplitude, one position (and the zone where inversion works)

Round 262.  Rounds 260 and 261 both scored themselves on runs that tuned them.  This round builds two
cases outside that set and scores the two estimators on them, with the same three gates pre-registered
before either case existed.

| hold-out | why it is held out |
|---|---|
| `h-a-150um-c58.5` | 150 µm is in neither calibration (the fit series has 120 and 200; the onset table has 120 and 200, so 150 interpolates that table rather than repeating its vertices) |
| `h-b-100um-c59.5` | every calibration run sits at 58.5 m; this one is 1 m away |

Each case: canonical `straight_one_set`, Gaussian bump on the blade body only, sigma 1.2 m, 80 km/h,
0.6 s, 1201 frames, gates 7/7.  54 and 48 blade stations touched respectively.

## 1. Results

```
hold-out              est      p95  worst %  loc err  gates
h-a-150um-c58.5     400.0   249.03    50.12    0.089  FAIL/FAIL/PASS   (share-only)
h-a-150um-c58.5     162.8    15.99     3.22    0.089  PASS/FAIL/PASS   (hybrid)
h-b-100um-c59.5     154.2    60.29    12.14    0.278  FAIL/FAIL/FAIL   (share-only)
h-b-100um-c59.5      67.6    36.77     7.41    0.278  FAIL/FAIL/FAIL   (hybrid)

share-only 0/2, hybrid 0/2 (the calibration series of round 261 gave 5/9)
```

Two estimators were scored, because round 261 showed they carry different information:

* **share-only** — the two-parameter forward fit (amplitude, centre) of round 261;
* **hybrid** — amplitude from the *onset station* through the round-257 linear map
  `A = (58.610 m − onset) / 3.2699 mm/µm`, centre from the share fit with the amplitude held.  The onset
  map is a property of the ramp and does not follow the bump (round 258: 67 mm of onset movement for a
  1 m shift), so it is allowed to set the amplitude only, never the position.

## 2. What the two hold-outs say

1. **The amplitude hold-out splits the two gates.**  The hybrid lands 162.8 µm against 150 µm
   (8.5 % high) and p95 **15.99 µm — inside the 22 µm absolute gate** — but the worst 2 mm bin is
   **3.22 %**, over the 2 % relative gate.  So the onset route does carry an amplitude above the share
   saturation, at roughly 8 % amplitude error, and that error is exactly what the relative gate refuses.
2. **A 1 m position shift breaks both.**  Share-only reads 154 µm for 100 µm and misses the position by
   0.278 m (outside the 0.20 m gate); the hybrid reads 67.6 µm (32 % low) and inherits the same
   position error.  Nothing here is rescued by pointing at the calibration series.
3. **The share fit saturates earlier where the blade is already loaded**, and that is the mechanism.
   At 58.5 m the canonical gap is −9 µm and the un-bumped share is 0.586; at 59.5 m the blade has risen
   further, the null share is higher, so 100 µm of raise pushes the share to 1.000 over a wider stretch
   and the two parameters stop being identifiable.  The estimator is not broken; the *observable* is
   gone.

## 3. The zone where amplitude inversion works

Putting rounds 257–262 together, with numbers:

| where | what can be inverted | evidence |
|---|---|---|
| ramp zone, `d(s) > 0` (up to ≈58.4 m), amplitudes ≤ 80 µm | amplitude to 0.3–1.5 %, profile to p95 1.4–7.7 µm | round 261, 5/9 |
| same zone, 150 µm | amplitude to 8.5 %, profile to p95 16 µm, relative error 3.2 % | this round |
| beyond the ramp (blade already carrying), any amplitude | position only (0.28 m here — and that also missed the 0.20 m gate) | this round |
| below 50 µm | nothing from the station observable (round 257: share never reaches 0.95) | round 257 |
| laterally | 4 mm per pass (round 259) | round 259 |

So the honest scope of "surface reconstruction" is: **on the ramp, for amplitudes between about 20 and
150 µm, with the absolute gate met and the relative gate not met above ~100 µm**.  Outside that zone the
estimators give position, not amplitude, and this round's 0.278 m shows even that is not automatic.

## 4. Boundaries

* Two hold-outs only; each is one shape (sigma 1.2 m), one speed, one side, one pass.
* The onset map was interpolated here, not extrapolated — a 250 µm hold-out would test extrapolation.
* The truth is synthetic: estimator test (class B), not a measurement of a real rail.
* Coverage unchanged: one pass sees 4 mm of the 37.15 mm head.
